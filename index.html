Xinyu Shao

I am a wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter (postdoc) at the Institut für Algebra, Zahlentheorie und Diskrete Mathematik, Leibniz Universität Hannover, in the p-adic geometry group of Prof. Dr. Ben Heuer.

Previously, I was a Ph.D. student at Sorbonne Université, within the Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche, under the supervision of Wiesława Nizioł.

Research interests: p-adic Hodge theory; arithmetic geometry; logarithmic geometry, anabelian geometry; p-adic local Langlands.
